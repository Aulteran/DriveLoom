What it is

One place for your vehicles, fuel and mileage, run on hardware you control instead of someone else's cloud.

Self-hosted

Runs on your own server. No account with a third party and no data leaving your instance.

Built for sharing

Several people can track the same vehicle, such as a family car or a shared van, in one history.

Open source

Licensed under AGPL-3.0, so you can read, audit and run exactly what you're trusting with your data.

Planned features

The direction for {{name}}. Details may change as it is built.

Fuel and odometer integrity

Entries are checked against each other so a mistyped odometer reading is caught before it skews your numbers.

Your units, everyone's units

Enter fuel and distance in whatever units you used at the pump. Each person views everything in the units they prefer.

Compare efficiency with friends

Friends on the same instance can compare how efficiently they drive, with the data they choose to share.

Flexible sign-in

Password sign-in, with optional single sign-on through OIDC, plus TOTP and passkeys for stronger accounts.

Your data, portable

Back up and restore a whole instance, migrate between servers, and export or import individual vehicles.

Fits your setup

Designed to sit behind your reverse proxy, such as Nginx Proxy Manager. No bundled tunnel, and you decide how it is exposed.

How it works

  1. Run it yourself

    Deploy {{name}} on a server you control and put it behind your own reverse proxy.

  2. Add your vehicles

    Create vehicles and invite the people who share them.

  3. Log as you go

    Record fill-ups and odometer readings in the units you actually used.

  4. See the real picture

    Watch consumption and cost trends, and compare with friends if you choose to.
