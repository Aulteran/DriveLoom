What it does

Built for real fill-ups, not perfect ones

People forget receipts, top up halfway and buy fuel abroad. {{name}} handles all of that without guessing.

Economy you can trust

Full-to-full intervals, with partial top-ups folded into the next full tank. A missed purchase leaves that interval out instead of skewing your average.

Any units in, your units out

Enter kilometres or miles, litres, US or imperial gallons. View everything in L/100 km, km/L or either mpg. What you typed is kept exactly.

Shared vehicles

One owner, plus editors who log and correct fill-ups and viewers who only read. Hand a car over by transferring ownership.

Compare with friends

Friends on your instance see only the averages of vehicles you opt in, never fill-ups, dates or notes. Turning sharing off takes effect at once.

Costs without guesswork

Each vehicle totals in one currency. A purchase in another currency needs your rate or the amount you were charged. No rate is ever assumed.

Portable data

Export a vehicle as CSV or JSON and import it on any instance. Imports preview first, skip duplicates and are all-or-nothing.

Backups that restore

Administrators take a full backup, photos included, in one checksummed file, and can restore it into a fresh instance.

Sign-in your way

Passwords, authenticator apps and passkeys. Registration is invite-only by default, and the first account needs a setup token.

Self-hosted

Your vehicle. Your data. Your server.

{{name}} is one Node process and a PostgreSQL database, started with Docker Compose. Put it behind the reverse proxy you already run; Caddy and nginx examples are included.

  • No telemetry, analytics or call home
  • Vehicle photos stay in your database
  • The database is never published to the network
  • AGPL-3.0, so you can read and audit every line

Read the self-hosting guide

# settings: public URL, secret key, passwords
cp deploy/.env.example deploy/.env

# build and start the app and database
docker compose -f deploy/compose.yml \
  --env-file deploy/.env up -d --build --wait

# then point your proxy at 127.0.0.1:8080

Questions

FAQ

Can I use it today?

The code is on GitHub and runs from source with Docker Compose. There is no tagged release yet and the security review is still to come, so treat it as a preview rather than something to trust with years of records.

Is the demo the real app?

It is a mock-up built with the app's own styles and running on sample data in your browser. The screens and rules match the app, but nothing talks to a server.

Does this site track me?

No. There are no analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests and no sign-up forms. The demo keeps everything in memory and forgets it when you leave.

Where does my data live?

On your own server. {{name}} is self-hosted, so there is no central service holding your vehicle history, and it never phones home.

Is it free?

It is open source under AGPL-3.0, so you can run it yourself at no cost beyond your own hosting.

Will there be phone apps?

Native Android and iPhone apps are planned for later, after the first release. They have not been started.
